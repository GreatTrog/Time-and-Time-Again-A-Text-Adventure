How to Play

This is an interactive story. Read the text and choose an option to advance the narrative. Your choices will affect Millie's inventory and the story's outcome.

Your choices shape the kind of person Millie becomes. Your actions will be remembered.

Saving: Your progress is saved automatically at the start of each new scene. You can continue from where you left off by refreshing the page.

Accessibility: Adjust font size, high contrast mode, and animations in the Settings menu. The game is fully navigable using a keyboard.
